How to post

  • Times are Eastern, with Pacific alongside, matched to when your recent posts went out. Shift them to your busiest hours in Instagram Insights.
  • Carousels: upload the images in numbered order on Instagram and Facebook. On LinkedIn, upload the PDF as a document so nothing gets cropped.
  • Links: Instagram points to the link in bio. LinkedIn links go in the first comment. Facebook captions include the full link.
  • Alt text is written for every post (Instagram: Advanced settings, then Accessibility).
