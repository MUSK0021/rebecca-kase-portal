Photos are uploaded far larger than they are shown

Fix now

Many photos are saved as large PNG files of 1 to 1.5 MB each. A photo shown 500 pixels wide on screen does not need to be 1,500 to 2,400 pixels wide.

Where
  • Speaking: 8 images over 1 MB each
  • Explore My Work: 5 images over 1 MB
  • Homepage: 2 images over 1 MB, plus one at almost 1 MB
  • About: 2 images over 1 MB
How to fix
Before uploading, resize photos to the size they are shown (about 1,600 pixels wide for full-width banners, 800 for cards), save them as JPG or WebP, and run them through a free compressor such as Squoosh or TinyPNG. Aim for under 300 KB per image.

Tiny icons are uploaded as huge images

Fix now

The small icons on the homepage (shown about 50 pixels wide) are 2,000-pixel images. There are 10 of them on the homepage alone.

How to fix
Re-export the icons at 100 to 150 pixels wide, or as SVG files.

A pop-up image loads on 27 pages, even when nobody opens it

Fix soon

The "Get your copy today" pop-up includes a 1.3 MB image. The homepage, the blog, all 20 articles and several other pages download it on every visit, whether or not the pop-up is opened.

How to fix
Compress that one image to under 200 KB. It is also used in the homepage's "Start Here" section, so this single change speeds up 27 pages.

Plain-English glossary

Words used in this report

Broken link

A link that leads to a page that no longer exists, so the visitor sees "Page not found".

Page title

The blue headline Google shows for a page in search results. It is also the text on the browser tab.

Page description

The one- or two-line summary under the title in Google results. Also called a meta description.

Main heading (H1)

The single biggest heading on a page. Google uses it to understand what the page is about.

Alt text

A short written description of an image, read aloud by screen readers and used by Google.

Page size

How much a browser downloads to show a page, measured in megabytes (MB). Smaller pages load faster.

Contrast

How much text stands out from its background. Low contrast is hard to read, especially on a phone in daylight.

Sitemap

A list of all your pages that helps Google find them. Yours is at rebeccakase.com/sitemap.xml.

Structured data

Hidden labels in the page code that tell Google facts directly, such as your name, books and profiles.

Google Lighthouse

Google's free checking tool. It grades a page on speed, accessibility, search basics and best practice.

Layout shift

When content jumps around while a page is still loading.

reCAPTCHA

Google's spam checker for forms, shown as a small badge in the corner of the screen.
